Over the years, the organisation has operated under several names:

SkillShare

Launched in the late 1980s, the Skillshare program targeted unemployed individuals who were disadvantaged in the labor market, equipping them with local trade, administration, and job-readiness skills.

Originally founded in the early 1990s, the centre first launched as part of the Australian Government's "SkillShare" program. Its primary objective was to provide skills training, support, and employment opportunities to the local unemployed workforce. Maryborough was targetted by this program due to closure of the knitting mill, flour mill, butter factory and other signficant employers in Maryborough.

Goldfields Employment and Learning Centre

Reflecting a wider regional reach across the Goldfields district, this iteration unified employment assistance with lifelong learning workshops.

Its primary mission during this decade was providing a "second chance at learning". It targeted youth, the unemployed, and individuals facing social, financial, or health barriers in the regional Central Goldfields area. During this period, GELC focused heavily on job placement services, basic literacy and numeracy programs (such as ACFE initiatives), and early computer/internet training to bridge the regional digital divide.

Mill House Neighbourhood House

Goldfields Employment and Learning Centre Inc (GELC) now trades as Mill House Neighbourhood House.Mill House has evolved from an employment services facility to a Neighbourhood House Model, focusing on Community development, social inclusion, and life skills. Mill House has enriched the local community by fostering social connectedness, promoting healthy lifestyles, and providing learning and recreational opportunities in an inclusive, safe, and welcoming environment.

24/7 Community Food Pantry

Our Community Food Pantry is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Members of the Central Goldfields community can access free food and essential items that have been generously donated by local businesses, organisations and community members.

We ask everyone to read the guidelines displayed on the pantry and only take what they need so there is enough to share with others.

Cool Spaces

Mill House opens as a Cool Space on days when the temperature is forecast to reach more than 38°C.

A Cool Space is an air-conditioned indoor location where people can find relief during periods of extreme heat. It is particularly important for older people, young children, people living with disability or health conditions, and anyone without reliable access to air conditioning.

During hot weather, remember to check that your family, friends, neighbours and pets are keeping cool and hydrated.

Onsite Employment Service Providers

Mill House provides private and professional spaces for employment service providers to meet with local residents. Our visiting employment service providers can assist people with disability to explore employment opportunities, identify their strengths and interests, develop skills, prepare resumes and job applications, and connect with suitable employers. Whether you're looking for your first job, returning to work, seeking more suitable employment or wanting to build your confidence and skills, support is available to help you take the next step.

Contact the service providers directly to discuss your needs or to arrange an appointment.

If you want to find out more about a particular employment service provider the click on their logo.

Digital Access Hub

Helping the community with general technology support.

Our digital access hub provides community members with access to technology, internet and support to build digital confidence.

  • Desktop and laptop computers for community use.
  • Free WiFi access.
  • Printing and scanning facilities.
  • Accessibility features to support diffewrent needs.
  • Friendly assistance from digital mentors.
  • A meeting room with conference camera equipment for online meetings, video calls and hybrid meetings.

Whether you need to get online to complete a task, attend an online appointment or meeting, learn something new or build your digital skills, the Mill house digital access hub is here to help.

Our digital mentors can provide friendly support for people who wuld like help using devices, accessing online services or becoming more confident with technology.

Laptops are available for community use, so please ask about them at reception.

Meeting room and conference room equipment are available, so please contact Mill House for bookings or more information.
